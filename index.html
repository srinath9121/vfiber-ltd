About Venkateswara
Fiber Technologies

Venkateswara Fiber Technologies Private Limited (VF Technologies) provides high-precision backend engineering, planning, and deployment design services for telecom operators and broadband providers. We work behind the scenes of modern connectivity — turning field information into engineering decisions, construction-ready designs, and deployable network solutions.

  • ✓ High-Precision Make-Ready & Pole Loading (O-Calc Pro)
  • ✓ Construction-Ready AutoCAD Plansets & CD Packages
  • ✓ Katapult Pro Photogrammetry Processing & Make-Ready Modeling
  • ✓ Right-of-Way (ROW) & Municipal Permitting
  • ✓ Aerial & Underground OSP Fiber Routing
  • ✓ Dedicated US Time-Zone Alignment

Deep B2B Engineering Services

01

Research & Permitting

End-to-end jurisdictional research, right-of-way verification, and permit package preparation for utility pole attachments and fiber route deployments.

  • Right-of-Way (ROW) verification & landowner contact documentation
  • DOT, Railroad, Municipal, and Tribal authority permits
  • Utility pole ownership audits & joint-use applications
  • Permit package preparation with engineering drawings
  • Make-ready coordination with pole owners

Design / Drafting

OSP Fiber

End-to-end Outside Plant FTTH/FTTx network architecture — from aerial route optimization to splice closure diagrams and optical link loss budgeting.

  • End-to-end FTTH / FTTx network architecture design
  • Aerial & buried route optimization with mid-span clearance validation
  • Splice closure diagrams & optical link loss budget calculations
  • ODF distribution panel mapping & fiber count documentation
  • Make-ready engineering — pole transfer, guying, and riser design

04

Pole Loading Analysis (O-Calc Pro)

NESC-compliant 3D structural digital twin modeling, wind/ice load calculations, and pass/fail structural certification reports using O-Calc Pro.

  • 3D structural digital twin modeling of utility pole assemblies
  • NESC Grade B/C & GO-95 wind and ice load vector calculations
  • Guying & anchor sizing analysis for overloaded structures
  • Pass/fail structural certification reports for pole make-ready
  • Make-ready remediation design — riser height, transfer, replacement specs
